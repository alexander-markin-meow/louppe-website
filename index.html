louppe media culler

free photo, video & audio culling • local • keyboard-first • macOS

open a folder or memory card, review with the keyboard, sort and clean up, then export your keepers. no catalog import needed.

from folder to keepers

  • RAW+JPEG grouping
    optionally review matching RAW+JPEG files as one photo.
  • keyboard-driven review
    review in Gallery or Grid with the keyboard. mark decisions, add stars and color labels, filter, sort and select multiple items.
  • look closer, listen back
    true 100% zoom, video and audio playback, a histogram and clipping information. optional quality cues flag high ISO, slow shutter speeds and dark or bright areas.
  • review duplicates and bursts
    find exact duplicates, likely similar photos and capture bursts locally on your Mac. analysis never changes ratings or files.
  • clean up with control
    send rejects to the macOS Trash, or keep only the RAW or JPEG from matching pairs. undo during the open session while files remain in Trash.
  • organize
    Organize Source Folder arranges media by date, rating or camera. preview and confirm each plan; undo during the open session.
  • export your keepers
    copy by default, move on the same drive, or route copies to multiple folders. use XMP sidecars for portable decisions, stars and labels in compatible editing apps.
  • local, open and private
    your media stays local. the app has no account or telemetry and is free and open source under the MIT License.

requirements and first run

  • macOS 14 or later
    the current download is for Apple silicon. Intel Macs are not supported by this build.
  • easy first run
    unzip Louppe.zip and drag Louppe into Applications. open it normally; macOS may ask you to confirm the first launch.
