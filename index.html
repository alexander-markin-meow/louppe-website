louppe

free photo & video culler • local • keyboard-first • macOS

review a folder or memory card, choose what to keep, then export without importing into a catalog

features

  • fully keyboard workflow
    f = keep, d = cull, arrows to move, space to play or pause video
  • photo and video, including raw
    common camera raw files including NEF, RAF, CR2 and ARW, plus JPEG, TIFF, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, and macOS-supported video
  • XMP sidecar export
    write ratings and labels as XMP sidecars for Lightroom, Bridge, Capture One, and darktable
  • metadata filters and close inspection
    filter by camera, lens, settings, date, file type, media type, or video length; inspect at true 100%, with histogram and clipping warnings
  • safe exports and cleanup
    copy keepers to a new folder by default, or deliberately move files and send rejects to the macOS Trash
  • local, open, and private
    100% local, with no account and no telemetry. free and open source under the MIT License.

requirements and first run

  • macOS 14 or later
    the current download is for Apple silicon. Intel Macs are not supported by this build.
  • one small first-run step
    unzip Louppe.zip and drag Louppe into Applications. because the app is not notarized yet, right-click it, choose Open, then choose Open again the first time.
