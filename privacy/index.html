louppe media culler

privacy policy

effective 31 august 2026

the app

  • your media stays on your Mac
    Louppe does not upload photographs, video, audio, ratings, metadata, file names, or folder locations. it has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics, or telemetry.
  • folder access is user controlled
    Louppe can read or change files only in folders you explicitly choose through macOS. the Mac App Store edition stores security-scoped bookmarks locally so recent folders and interrupted file operations can be reopened safely. these bookmarks never leave your Mac.
  • local records
    ratings and labels are stored in the selected folder’s .louppe_session.json file and in a local recovery backup. thumbnails, preferences, folder bookmarks, and crash-safe operation journals stay in Louppe’s app container. removing the app’s container removes its private local records; sidecars in your chosen folders remain under your control.
  • updates and external links
    the Mac App Store edition receives updates from Apple and makes no update-checking connection itself. the direct-download edition checks the public GitHub release feed through Sparkle, which sends a normal internet request to GitHub. links to the website, GitHub, or email open in your chosen external app.

the website

louppe.eu uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate visits and page use. Google may process device, browser, approximate location, and interaction information according to its own policies and the site’s analytics configuration. this website measurement is separate from the Louppe app, which does not contain Google Analytics.

sharing and sale

the app does not collect personal data, so there is no app data to sell, rent, or share. files are transferred only when you explicitly copy, move, organize, export, or send them to the macOS Trash.

changes and contact

this page will be updated if Louppe’s privacy practices change. questions can be sent to a@alex-markin.com.
